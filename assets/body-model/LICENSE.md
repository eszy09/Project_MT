# Body model asset license

## Artistic source

`source/project-mt-body-v1.fbx` is an original, deterministic Project_MT asset
generated from geometric primitives by
`scripts/body-model/generate-source-fbx.mjs`. It does not incorporate a
third-party artistic model or texture.

The source FBX and its generated GLB output are distributed under the same ISC
license declared by the root Project_MT package. Copyright belongs to the
Project_MT maintainers.
