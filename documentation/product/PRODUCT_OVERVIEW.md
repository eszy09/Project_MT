# Project_MT Product Overview

## Product statement

Project_MT is a responsive, visual-first strength-training and body-recomposition web application. It combines trustworthy workout logging, body measurement tracking, an interactive 3D body model, and explainable guidance.

The product should answer:

- What should I train?
- What did I do last time?
- Am I progressing toward my goal?

## Primary experience

1. Create an account.
2. Select a goal and target body areas.
3. Record initial measurements.
4. View an approximate personalized body model.
5. Select a muscle or routine.
6. Log a workout set by set.
7. Compare the session with previous performance.
8. Record periodic check-ins.
9. View progress trends and understandable recommendations.

## Primary users

### Visual progress seekers

Users motivated by body changes, measurement trends, and a visual representation of progress.

### Strength-training loggers

Users who need fast set entry, previous values, reusable routines, and reliable history.

### Consistency builders

Users who benefit from one clear recommended action and an understandable adherence signal.

## V1 capabilities

- Authentication and onboarding
- Goals and target areas
- Interactive 3D muscle selection
- Set-by-set workout logging
- Workout history and comparisons
- Reusable routines
- Body measurements and check-ins
- Progress trends
- Explainable goal-proximity indicator
- Private journal
- Profile, privacy, export, and deletion controls

## Later capabilities

- Recovery, activity, and nutrition context
- Secure progress photos and body-composition reports
- Wearable integrations
- Rule-based adaptive recommendations
- Public community features only after moderation and privacy systems exist
- AI guidance only after sufficient reliable historical data exists

## Non-goals

- Medical diagnosis or treatment
- Clinical-grade body composition
- Guaranteed spot-fat reduction
- Automatic clinical 3D reconstruction
- Fully autonomous AI coaching
- Unmoderated public posting

## Product principles

- Logging must be durable and exact.
- Privacy is the default.
- The body model is an approximation and interaction surface.
- Recommendations must explain their inputs.
- Common logging actions must be fast on mobile.
- Health language must be honest and non-diagnostic.
- Critical actions must not require the 3D model; accessible alternatives are required.

## Initial success milestone

> A user can create an account, complete onboarding, create or copy a workout, log every set, save it, restart the system, sign back in, and retrieve exactly the same data.
